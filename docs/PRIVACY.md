# Privacy Policy for Fontokmai (ฝนตกไหม)

**Last Updated:** October 16, 2026

## 1. Overview
Fontokmai ("ฝนตกไหม", "we", "our", or "the app") is an intelligent AI commute and flood resilience platform designed to help Bangkok residents navigate urban flooding risks. This Privacy Policy discloses our practices concerning data collection, processing, and protection.

## 2. Google User Data & Google Calendar Access
Fontokmai offers an optional feature enabling users to link their Google Calendar account to anticipate commute departure schedules.

- **OAuth Scope Requested:** `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly`
- **Specific Data Accessed:** We access strictly read-only event timestamps (start and end times) and destination address/location strings for upcoming events. We do **not** access event descriptions, attendee emails, notes, attachments, or full calendars.
- **Purpose of Access:** Event locations and times are used exclusively to calculate departure timing and route safety against live Bangkok Metropolitan Administration (BMA) road flood sensors, canal tidal surge predictions, and WeatherNext 3 radar precipitation forecasts.

### Google Limited Use Disclosure
> Fontokmai's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the **Limited Use** requirements.

## 3. Data Retention and Minimization
- **In-Memory Processing:** Calendar event data and route coordinates are held ephemerally in-memory during the active planning session.
- **No Persistent Database Storage:** Google user data, calendar schedules, and historical locations are never saved to persistent backend databases or disk storage.
- **Zero Third-Party Sharing:** We do **not** sell, rent, monetize, or transfer your Google user data or personal information to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers.

## 4. User Control & Access Revocation
You can disconnect calendar access at any time directly in the application settings, or revoke access immediately through your Google Account Security Dashboard:
👉 [https://myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions)
